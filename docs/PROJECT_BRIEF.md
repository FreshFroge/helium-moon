# 5. THE HELIUM VOICE TERROR RADIUS

This is a REQUIRED gameplay system.

Helium Voice emits an invisible supernatural terror radius.

The effects become stronger as the player approaches him.

The player should be able to sense the danger before they can see him.

The following distances are initial balancing values and should be configurable.

### OUTER RING — 80 to 120 meters

**THE AIR BEGINS TO GET COLDER.**

This is the signature effect I specifically want.

As a player enters the outer ring:
- Ambient temperature drops.
- The surrounding atmosphere becomes noticeably colder.
- The player may start seeing faint condensation from their breath.
- A subtle cold wind becomes audible.
- The general forest ambience becomes slightly quieter.
- Very faint, distant supernatural audio may begin.

There should NOT be an obvious flashing HUD notification announcing that the monster is nearby.

Players should gradually learn that unexplained cold means Helium Voice is close.

### MIDDLE RING — 40 to 80 meters

The environmental effects intensify:
- The player breathes more heavily.
- Condensation becomes more visible.
- Subtle high-frequency sound distortion begins.
- Wildlife noise decreases.
- Light fog becomes more apparent.
- Distant whispers may be audible.
- Small camera tremors may occur at irregular intervals.
- The flashlight may occasionally fluctuate in brightness.

The player should become increasingly anxious without necessarily seeing the entity.

### INNER RING — 15 to 40 meters

The danger becomes serious.

Effects may include:
- More pronounced breathing.
- Faster heartbeat.
- Stronger atmospheric cold.
- Distorted environmental audio.
- Brief peripheral visual anomalies.
- An increasing sense of psychological pressure.

Helium Voice may begin actively stalking or tracking the player.

### CRITICAL RANGE — Under 15 meters

At this distance:
- The player is in immediate danger.
- Helium Voice may attack or initiate a chase.
- The audio becomes extremely tense.
- The player's breathing and heartbeat become prominent.
- The entity may emerge from darkness or nearby cover.

However, do not automatically trigger an unavoidable jump scare every time a player enters this distance.

The entity must behave according to actual AI, visibility, environmental obstacles, and player actions.

### Technical requirement

Implement the terror radius as a real continuously evaluated 3D distance system with smooth transitions.

Use audio, visual effects, temperature variables, and AI reactions driven by that system.

The terror radius must work correctly for EACH player in multiplayer.
