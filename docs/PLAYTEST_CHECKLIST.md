# Helium Moon playtest checklist (for the owner)

Each session takes about 15–25 minutes. Use headphones, and play somewhere dark if
you can. Write your answers in a reply to Claude or Codex, or add them to the
"Your answers" section at the bottom (any format is fine). Scripted tests can't
tell us whether it's *scary* or *fun*, so your answers decide the next changes.
Background numbers are in [`PACING_REPORT.md`](PACING_REPORT.md).

**Get the current build:** download this branch (`codex/early-build-playtest`)
as a ZIP from GitHub, unzip it and double-click `start-game.cmd` (or run
`node server/server.js`, then open http://localhost:8080). The hosted page may
lag behind this branch. Check the commit it says it was built from.

Press **F3** during play to see the performance overlay, including the frame rate.

---

## Run 1: play normally (do this first, before reading the rest)

Play the game as you naturally would: read the memo, do the three objectives
and try to escape.

1. How long until you first felt uneasy? What caused it (cold, a sound, a shape)?
2. Did you ever see Helium Voice clearly? Did it feel earned or sudden?
3. Did he attack you? If yes, did you understand what you did wrong?
4. Was there a stretch where you were bored? Roughly when, and for how long?
5. Did the skunk ever surprise you? Did the gas feel fair?
6. Frame rate and quality setting you used (from F3 and Settings).

## Run 2: never look back

Walk the trail toward the objectives and **don't turn around**, whatever you hear.

- Our tests say Helium Voice can't hit you this way. He slows to a creep at
  about 2.4 m and only attacks once he's on your screen. That matches your
  earlier instruction.
- **Question:** is that how you want it? Should a player who refuses to look be
  safe from him, or should he eventually close in and grab them?

## Run 3: glance back often

Same as Run 2, but look behind you every 15–20 seconds.

- **Question:** when you caught him close behind you and he lunged, did the
  attack feel scary and fair, or cheap? Was the reverse crawl escape creepy?

## Run 4: hide

Crouch in thick brush off the trail with your flashlight off for a few minutes.

- In our tests he never got on screen for a hidden player, but the skunk found
  them in 2 of 4 runs.
- **Question:** was hiding tense, or just quiet? Should hiding be safer, or
  riskier?

## Run 5: co-op (if you can get a second player)

One player lags 30 m behind the other.

- **Question:** did the straggler feel more hunted? Did you hear your own or
  your friend's voice in the trees? (That needs the microphone and the mimicry
  opt-in in Settings, and only works in the downloaded version.)

---

## The three decisions we need from you

1. **Blind fleeing:** safe, or eventually caught?
2. **The quiet middle:** the tests show long stretches (often 8–15 minutes)
   with no threat after the first sighting. Does that feel like dread, or like
   nothing happening?
3. **First contact:** he usually shows up within seconds of the first cold.
   Should the cold warning come earlier, or should his first appearance be
   delayed so the dread builds longer?
