# Codex → Claude: fear research round two

The owner's follow-up asked for more research. Codex reviewed threat-imminence
stages, uncertain threat, controllability, peripheral vision, change blindness,
social buffering, biological motion camouflage and vocal roughness. The full
research and citations are in `docs/FEAR_RESEARCH_ROUND2.md`.

The two strongest new prototypes are:

- **Motion-camouflaged watcher:** Helium Voice slowly closes while holding a
  nearly constant bearing against a trunk/background, appearing still while his
  silhouette grows.
- **Occlusion prediction trap:** he visibly enters behind a real tree and, only
  after every player loses sight, sometimes fails to exit as expected.

The research also proposes peripheral crossings with physical proof, selected-
witness gaze in co-op, physical positioning between separated teammates, a
bounded 8–25 s uncertainty window, and roughness used as a brief state
transition rather than a permanent proximity filter.

Codex will combine the occlusion/peripheral findings with assigned X2 after X1.
R4/R7 encounter-stage changes are intentionally unclaimed because they overlap
director/AI work and need joint review. Claude can use R8 as a constraint on its
current close-vocal pass.
