# October 26 gameplay pass

Owner priorities: timed hunt search first, repeated playthroughs, trailhead,
then objectives. Changes are on `codex/early-build-playtest`; the shared
branch includes Claude's `74a4fbb` and `38f1896` fixes.

## Faults found and changes

| Fault / observation | Resulting change |
| --- | --- |
| A no-grab timer still let pursuit overlap the player's body; search could learn the current player position through cover | Separate distant stalk and evidence-based search. Opening movement cannot close through a player's body. No live-coordinate reacquisition, hunt slip, or cutoff teleport. |
| Hunts could run indefinitely; hiding could instead cancel one just six seconds into its opening | One sampled deadline: 15–20 s stalk, 30–40 s search. Discovery permits a rush; contact does not reset the deadline. Timed-out grabs release without late damage; survived hunts receive recovery grace. |
| Initial kiosk, path entrance and ground were hard to read in actual rendered views | One sheltered warm kiosk lamp, map frame and landmark labels, trail-entry waypost, collision-backed seating, irregular gravel edges and batched stone/leaf dressing. All six spawn routes remain clear. |
| Gas/fuses could be on unlabelled patches of ground with no clue | Required supplies now occupy named landmarks, randomized each session. The kiosk memo and inventory supply register name their actual storage sites. |
| Four future objectives cluttered the first view; no carried trail map | Initial HUD points to the memo; afterward it shows up to two available repairs. Hold Tab for a marked East Trail map, your position, landmark legend, full rescue plan and clues. |
| A campsite note could land beside the trailhead memo, with a misleading prompt | Reserve the kiosk for the briefing. Displaced campsite notes are described as crumpled notes rather than pinned to a nonexistent tent. |
| Leaving a repair to hide gradually erased completed work | Generator, relay and transmitter have three described steps. Each completed third latches permanently; unfinished work only recedes to that checkpoint. Nearby helpers accelerate work, capped at 1.7×. |
| Client rounding made the saved first third display as step 1 again | Account for .001 snapshot precision; the actual browser now resumes at step 2 after interruption. |
| Server interaction accepted some occluded or held-player actions | Validate line of sight, height and range; held or covered players cannot repair equipment. |

The rescue goal remains generator + trail relay → distress call → highway
extraction. This pass adds direction and interruption choices rather than
replacing the scenario.

## Full-session simulations

`HUNT_SESSIONS=12 HUNT_MINUTES=25 HUNT_JSON=<path> node tests/hunt_playtests.mjs`
was run twice: after the search change, and after the objective change. Bots
follow the real objective chain and authoritative simulation, but know item
locations. They walk/run and revive teammates; their legacy strategies do not
exercise the new hiding counterplay like a human would.

| Measurement | After hunt change | After objectives |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Sessions | 12 | 12 |
| Hunts | 63 | 56 |
| Longest hunt | 57.15 s | 57.40 s |
| Escapes | 5 | 6 |
| Losses | 4 | 4 |
| Time-outs | 3 | 2 |

These are modest samples with layout-driven RNG changes, not proof of a
causal win-rate improvement or final difficulty. The new clues and map need
human navigation feedback. Perceptual scare quality and frame rate need a
real-GPU play session; headless software rendering cannot establish either.
